**Which file:** play `01_Films/Brightpost_Walkthrough_Captions_1080p60.mp4` and turn the music down or off, so you can talk over it. The on-screen captions carry the key points if the room cannot hear you well. Do not talk over the voiceover version.

**If asked afterwards**

- *Is the shop real?* No. Harbor Bikes and its logo were made for this demo. The photos and Brightpost logos come from the brand hub.
- *Does it make up pictures?* No. It only uses photos from the brand hub. It writes the text and lays out the design.
- *Can it publish for the shop?* No. The shop owner downloads the images and copies the text. The app reminds them to check before publishing.
- *How long does it take?* In this recording: 56 seconds for the first three options, 34 seconds for the redesign, 27 seconds for the German version, 118 seconds for the second campaign. Adding platforms and editing text are instant.
