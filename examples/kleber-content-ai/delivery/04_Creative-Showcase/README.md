# Creative showcase

Real output from Kleber Content AI: curated campaign images, ready to show.

## What is here

- `Kleber-Content-AI_Creative-Showcase.pdf`: all sheets in one PDF (page 1 is A3 landscape for print; pages 2 to 6 are 16:9 slides).
- `sheets/`: each sheet as a PNG (full quality) and a JPG (smaller file). The 16:9 sheets are 3840 × 2160 pixels and fit a slide edge to edge. The A3 poster is 4961 × 3508 pixels (A3 at 300 dpi).

| Sheet | Point it makes |
|---|---|
| `01_Hero_A3` / `01_Hero_16x9` | Here is what it makes: a mix of dealers, languages, occasions and formats. |
| `02_One-option-18-formats` | One option in all 18 formats of the six platforms. |
| `03_Three-options-one-sentence` | The three options the app returned for the film's first sentence. |
| `04_Dealer-languages` | One post translated on request (English to German), next to posts briefed in French and Italian, with their post text. |
| `05_Before-and-after-redesign` | The same post before and after "Try a new design: photo on top, text in a red block underneath." |

- `creatives/`: 222 individual images at full resolution, in 15 folders, one per campaign. File names read `<campaign>_<option>_<platform>-<format>_<language>`, for example `summit-winter-krisalp-hp3_option-1_instagram-feed-square_en.jpg`.
- `index.csv`: one row per image with file, campaign, product, option, platform, format, pixel size, language, dealer, occasion, headline and source. Opens in Excel or Numbers.

## Where the images come from

- **The film's campaigns (Summit Tyre Centre).** The final images from the recording, plus earlier states saved during it: as first generated, after the dealer's edits, right after the redesign and right after the German translation. The last two were re-rendered from saved copies of the app's database at those moments.
- **Other campaigns stored in the app** during development, for four more demo dealers. They were re-rendered with the current version of the app, so they show today's layouts. Re-rendering uses the stored design and makes no AI call.
- No new AI requests were made for this showcase.

All dealers are fictional demo accounts that ship with the app: Summit Tyre Centre (made for the film), Autohaus Müller, Garage Dupont, Gommista Rossi, and a demo profile with a "KI" badge. Their logos are invented monograms made for the demo. Photos, product images and Kleber logos come from Kleber's brand hub. The football posts use Kleber's own LOSC and TSG Hoffenheim partnership photos from the hub.

## How they were chosen

From about 1,300 rendered images, only those a brand designer would sign off were kept: strong photo, readable type, both logos present and readable, nothing cropped badly. Left out:

- layouts that leave a large empty band (common in some story and wide formats);
- text-only fallbacks with no photo;
- small product shots lost in the frame;
- dealer logos placed over lettering in Kleber campaign artwork;
- the Kleber logo placed directly on a red background, where its red stripes disappear;
- a dealer logo with too little contrast on its own tile (the Polish demo dealer, so Polish is not shown);
- Halloween campaigns (a theme the brand hub does not cover; used only to test the app's honesty).

These were design calls made by hand for this showcase.
