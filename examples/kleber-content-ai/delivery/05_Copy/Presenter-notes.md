**If asked afterwards**

- *Is the dealer real?* No. Summit Tyre Centre and its logo were made for this demo. The photos and Kleber logos come from Kleber's brand hub.
- *Does it make up pictures?* No. It only uses photos from Kleber's brand hub. It writes the text and lays out the design.
- *Can it publish for the dealer?* No. The dealer downloads the images and copies the text. The app reminds them to check before publishing.
- *How long does it take?* In this recording: 56 seconds for the first three options, 34 seconds for the redesign, 27 seconds for the German version, 118 seconds for the second campaign. Adding platforms and editing text are instant.
