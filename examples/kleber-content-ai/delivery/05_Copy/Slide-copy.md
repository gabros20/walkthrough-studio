## 7. It remembers

**Say it once. It remembers.**

Standing rules become saved notes the dealer can see and delete. A brand kit sets
their colour, button shape and logo order. The next campaign follows both.

Use: `Kleber-Content-AI_07_Teach-it-a-rule_Captions.mp4`, `Kleber-Content-AI_08_Brand-kit-and-memory_Captions.mp4` and `Kleber-Content-AI_09_Second-campaign-follows-the-rules_Captions.mp4`, or screenshots `Kleber-Content-AI_08d_Brand-kit_Saved-and-assistant-notes.png` and `Kleber-Content-AI_09b_Second-campaign_Sign-off-added-automatically.png`.

## 8. Ready to post

**Download. Paste. Post.**

Each option downloads as one kit: every image at its exact size, plus the post text.
The dealer stays in control; nothing is published without them.

Use: `Kleber-Content-AI_10_Export-the-kit_Captions.mp4`, or screenshot `Kleber-Content-AI_10_Export_Download-sheet.png`.
