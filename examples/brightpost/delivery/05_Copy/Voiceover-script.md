## Plain script (no times)

One sentence from a shop. A campaign, ready to post.

They join with their own one-time link.

Their logo, added once, goes on every post.

Then they describe the post in their own words.

Three on-brand options, from the brand hub.

Each image is checked before it's shown.

Three different ideas, side by side, to choose between.

Each comes sized for the feed, and for stories.

Every word can be edited, even on the image.

Want a change? Ask for it in plain words.

The layout changes. The brand rules don't.

Adding channels is instant. Nothing to wait for.

Six platforms, eighteen formats, all from one option.

Each carries the same message, and the same look.

The whole app speaks eight languages, not just the posts.

Posts translate too, with a single request.

German on the image and in the post, everywhere.

Some things only need saying once.

That's kept as a note, and added to every new request.

In settings, they set their own brand kit.

Each choice shows straight away on their latest campaign, and nothing changes until they save.

And here are the assistant's saved notes.

The next campaign follows the notes and the kit.

Their logo first, their colour, their button shape.

And their sign-off, added without asking again.

The whole kit downloads at once. Copy the text, and post.

Every shop, on brand, from one sentence to every channel.
