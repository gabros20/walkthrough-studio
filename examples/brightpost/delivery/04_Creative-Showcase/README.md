# Creative showcase

Real output from Brightpost: curated campaign images, ready to show.

## What is here

- `Brightpost_Creative-Showcase.pdf`: all sheets in one PDF (page 1 is A3 landscape for print; pages 2 to 6 are 16:9 slides).
- `sheets/`: each sheet as a PNG (full quality) and a JPG (smaller file). The 16:9 sheets are 3840 × 2160 pixels and fit a slide edge to edge. The A3 poster is 4961 × 3508 pixels (A3 at 300 dpi).

| Sheet | Point it makes |
|---|---|
| `01_Hero_A3` / `01_Hero_16x9` | Here is what it makes: a mix of shops, languages, occasions and formats. |
| `02_One-option-18-formats` | One option in all 18 formats of the six platforms. |
| `03_Three-options-one-sentence` | The three options the app returned for the film's first sentence. |
| `04_Shop-languages` | One post translated on request (English to German), next to posts briefed in French and Italian, with their post text. |
| `05_Before-and-after-redesign` | The same post before and after "Try a new design: photo on top, text in a teal block underneath." |

- `creatives/`: 222 individual images at full resolution, in 15 folders, one per campaign. File names read `<campaign>_<option>_<platform>-<format>_<language>`, for example `harbor-winter-ridgeline-7_option-1_instagram-feed-square_en.jpg`.
- `index.csv`: one row per image with file, campaign, product, option, platform, format, pixel size, language, shop, occasion, headline and source. Opens in Excel or Numbers.

## Where the images come from

- **The film's campaigns (Harbor Bikes).** The final images from the recording, plus earlier states saved during it: as first generated, after the shop owner's edits, right after the redesign and right after the German translation. The last two were re-rendered from saved copies of the app's database at those moments.
- **Other campaigns stored in the app** during development, for four more demo shops. They were re-rendered with the current version of the app, so they show today's layouts. Re-rendering uses the stored design and makes no AI call.
- No new AI requests were made for this showcase.

All shops are fictional demo accounts that ship with the app: Harbor Bikes (made for the film), Nordkap Bikes, Rivertown Cycles, Ciclisti Verdi, and a demo profile with a "KI" badge. Their logos are invented monograms made for the demo. Photos, product images and Brightpost logos come from the brand hub. The club-ride posts use the club-ride photos from the hub.

## How they were chosen

From about 1,300 rendered images, only those a brand designer would sign off were kept: strong photo, readable type, both logos present and readable, nothing cropped badly. Left out:

- layouts that leave a large empty band (common in some story and wide formats);
- text-only fallbacks with no photo;
- small product shots lost in the frame;
- shop logos placed over lettering in brand campaign artwork;
- the Brightpost logo placed directly on a teal background, where its teal symbol disappears;
- a shop logo with too little contrast on its own tile (the Polish demo shop, so Polish is not shown);
- Halloween campaigns (a theme the brand hub does not cover; used only to test the app's honesty).

These were design calls made by hand for this showcase.
