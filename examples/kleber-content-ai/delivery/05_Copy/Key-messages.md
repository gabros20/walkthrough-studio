# Key messages

## In one line

A Kleber dealer types one sentence, and Kleber Content AI returns ready-to-post, on-brand campaign images and post text for six platforms.

## In one paragraph

Kleber Content AI is a chat app for Kleber tyre dealers. The dealer describes the post they need in their own words, for example "First snow is forecast this weekend. Winter tyre switch posts for the Krisalp HP3." The app answers with three campaign options built only from Kleber's brand hub: hub photos, Kleber logos and brand colours, plus the dealer's own logo. Every image is checked before the dealer sees it. The dealer can edit any word, ask for changes in plain words, add platforms, switch language, then download the kit and post. The app remembers the dealer's rules and design choices, so the next campaign starts from them.

## Proof points, with the moment in the film

Times are minutes:seconds into the walkthrough film. Both versions (captions and voiceover) have the same picture, so the times apply to both.

1. **One sentence in, three options out.** The dealer types one sentence at 0:22. Three different options appear at 0:29, each built from a different hub photo. The app's own timer, "Worked for 56 s", is shown at about 0:37.
2. **Only Kleber material, checked before it is shown.** "Three on-brand options from Kleber's brand hub" (0:29) and "Every image is checked before you see it" (0:32). The app checks crops, contrast, safe areas and logos, and an AI reviewer looks at each option before it appears.
3. **Every word can be edited, even on the image.** At 0:53 the dealer clicks the headline on the picture and rewrites it. No new AI request is needed for that.
4. **Changes in plain words.** At 1:02 the dealer asks: "Try a new design: photo on top, text in a red block underneath." The new layout arrives at 1:13 ("Worked for 34 s"), with the same photo, words and brand rules.
5. **Six platforms, 18 formats, no waiting.** At 1:18 the dealer adds the other platforms from a menu, with no AI wait. From 1:21 the film tours all 18 formats: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, display ads and newsletter.
6. **It speaks the dealer's language.** The app itself runs in 8 languages (1:35). At 1:43 the dealer asks for the post in German; from 1:50 the image and the post text are both in German.
7. **Tell it once, and it remembers.** At 2:00 the dealer gives a standing rule (a sign-off line, and "closed on Sundays"); at 2:09 they set their own brand kit (colour, button shape, logo order). The next campaign follows both: dealer logo first, red style, pill button (2:40) and the sign-off added without asking (2:45). The whole kit downloads in one click at 2:51.

## Words to use and to avoid

- Say "options", "creatives" or "posts"; say "Kleber's brand hub" for the source material.
- Say "checked" or "reviewed", not "guaranteed" or "approved". The app checks each image; a person should still look before publishing (the app says so under the chat box: "Kleber Content AI can make mistakes. Check before you publish.").
- The dealer in the film, Summit Tyre Centre, is fictional. Do not present it as a real customer.
