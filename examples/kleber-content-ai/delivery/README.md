# Kleber Content AI: demo package

*November 2026. A nicer-looking copy of this page is `README.html` (opens in any browser) and `README.pdf`.*

## What Kleber Content AI is

Kleber Content AI is a chat app for Kleber tyre dealers. A dealer types one sentence, such as "First snow is forecast this weekend. Winter tyre switch posts for the Krisalp HP3.", and gets three on-brand campaign options with images and post text, ready for six platforms. Everything is built from Kleber's own brand hub, checked before it is shown, and downloaded as one kit the dealer can post straight away.

## What is in this package

| Folder | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `01_Films/` | Two versions of the same 3-minute walkthrough (3 min 2 s, 1920 × 1080). **Captions:** short on-screen labels with music. **Voiceover:** a narrator with music, no on-screen labels. Each has subtitle files (`.srt`, `.vtt`) and a poster image (`_poster_1920x1080.jpg`). Masters are 60 fps. | Showing the whole story: meetings, the client's intranet, a website. |
| `01_Films/web/` | Lighter copies of both films at 1080p and 720p, 30 fps (17 to 27 MB instead of about 89 MB). | Web pages, email links, slides that must stay small. |
| `02_Feature-Clips/` | The film cut into 12 short clips, one per feature (7 to 24 s each), in a `captions/` and a `voiceover/` set. Numbered in film order, from `00_Title` to `11_End-card`. | One clip per slide in a deck. |
| `03_Screenshots/` | 26 stills from the film at its key moments, cropped to the app window. `full-frame-16x9/` holds the same frames uncropped at 1920 × 1080. | Slides, documents, a web page, printed handouts. |
| `04_Creative-Showcase/` | Sheets of the best images the app made (one A3 poster, five 16:9 slides, also as one PDF), plus 222 individual images in 15 campaign folders with an `index.csv`. See its own `README.md`. | Showing the output itself; print the A3 poster. |
| `05_Copy/` | Ready-to-use text: key messages, facts and figures (each checked in the app's code), the film captions and voiceover script with times, presenter notes and slide copy. | Writing a deck, a web page or an email; presenting live. |

## How to use it

**In a PowerPoint or Keynote deck.** For the whole film, insert `01_Films/web/…_Captions_1080p30_web.mp4`; it plays without sound if needed, because the captions carry the message. For one slide per feature, insert the matching clip from `02_Feature-Clips/captions/`; `05_Copy/Slide-copy.md` has a headline and body for each slide and names the clip to use. For still slides, use `03_Screenshots/` or the 16:9 sheets in `04_Creative-Showcase/sheets/` (3840 × 2160, they fill a slide edge to edge).

**On a web page.** Use a `web` file (720p for speed, 1080p for quality), the matching `_poster_1920x1080.jpg` as the poster image, and the `.vtt` file as the subtitle track. With the voiceover version, the `.vtt` gives viewers subtitles for the narration.

**Presenting live.** Play the captions master with the music down and talk over it; `05_Copy/Presenter-notes.md` has a scene-by-scene talk track and answers to likely questions. Or play the voiceover version and let it speak for itself.

**Quoting numbers.** Take them from `05_Copy/Facts-and-figures.md`, where each one is tied to the app's code or the recording.

## Please note

- **The dealer is fictional.** "Summit Tyre Centre" and its logo were made for this demo, and so are the other dealers in the showcase. The creatives were generated by the app from Kleber's brand hub material.
- **The films show real app footage.** Waits for the assistant are shortened and shown as labelled time-lapses. The on-screen "Worked for N s" line shows the true duration.
- **Open check for the owner: music and voiceover licence.** The background music and the voiceover were generated with ElevenLabs on the owner's account. Before any public or commercial use, the owner should confirm the licence terms of the plan they were generated under. These terms have not been checked yet.
