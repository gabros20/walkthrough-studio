# Slide copy

Ready-to-paste text for about eight slides: a headline and a two-line body each, with the clip, screenshot or sheet that goes with it. Clips are in `02_Feature-Clips/` (pick `captions/` for silent slides with on-screen labels, `voiceover/` if the room should hear the narration). Screenshots are in `03_Screenshots/`. Sheets are in `04_Creative-Showcase/sheets/`.

---

## 1. Title

**One sentence in. A ready-to-post campaign out.**

Brightpost turns a shop owner's own words into on-brand posts for six platforms.
Built only from the brand hub.

Use: `04_Creative-Showcase/sheets/Brightpost_Showcase_01_Hero_16x9.png`, or the clip `02_Feature-Clips/captions/Brightpost_00_Title_Captions.mp4`.

---

## 2. Set up once

**Set up in a minute**

The shop owner confirms their company and adds their logo once.
From then on, it goes on every post, co-branded with Brightpost.

Use: `Brightpost_01_Set-up-once_Captions.mp4`, or screenshot `Brightpost_01b_Set-up_Logo-saved.png`.

---

## 3. Ask in plain words

**Describe the post. Get three options.**

One sentence is the whole brief. The assistant picks photos from the brand hub
and returns three different options, each checked before it is shown.

Use: `Brightpost_02_One-sentence-three-options_Captions.mp4`, or screenshot `Brightpost_02b_Ask_Three-options-to-choose-from.png`, or sheet `Brightpost_Showcase_03_Three-options-one-sentence.png`.

---

## 4. Edit and refine

**Change anything, in plain words**

Click any word to edit it, even on the image. Or ask for a new layout
in one sentence: the design changes, the brand rules stay.

Use: `Brightpost_03_Choose-and-edit-on-the-image_Captions.mp4` and `Brightpost_04_New-layout-by-request_Captions.mp4`, or sheet `Brightpost_Showcase_05_Before-and-after-redesign.png`.

---

## 5. Every channel

**6 platforms. 18 formats. One option.**

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, display ads and newsletter.
Adding a platform is instant, and every size is laid out for its own shape.

Use: `Brightpost_05_Six-platforms-eighteen-formats_Captions.mp4`, or sheet `Brightpost_Showcase_02_One-option-18-formats.png`.

---

## 6. Every language

**The shop's language, on the image and in the post**

The app itself runs in eight languages. Posts can be written in the shop's
language, or translated on request, words on the image included.

Use: `Brightpost_06_Languages-and-translation_Captions.mp4`, or sheet `Brightpost_Showcase_04_Shop-languages.png`, or screenshot `Brightpost_06a_Languages_German-post.png`.

---

## 7. It remembers

**Say it once. It remembers.**

Standing rules become saved notes the shop owner can see and delete. A brand kit sets
their colour, button shape and logo order. The next campaign follows both.

Use: `Brightpost_07_Teach-it-a-rule_Captions.mp4`, `Brightpost_08_Brand-kit-and-memory_Captions.mp4` and `Brightpost_09_Second-campaign-follows-the-rules_Captions.mp4`, or screenshots `Brightpost_08d_Brand-kit_Saved-and-assistant-notes.png` and `Brightpost_09b_Second-campaign_Sign-off-added-automatically.png`.

---

## 8. Ready to post

**Download. Paste. Post.**

Each option downloads as one kit: every image at its exact size, plus the post text.
The shop owner stays in control; nothing is published without them.

Use: `Brightpost_10_Export-the-kit_Captions.mp4`, or screenshot `Brightpost_10_Export_Download-sheet.png`.
